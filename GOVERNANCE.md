# Governance

| Role | Who | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder** | @m3di | Everything. Sole Owner of the `ganjine-dev` organization. Grants and revokes every role. Cannot be removed. |
| **Maintainer** | Listed in `MAINTAINERS.md` | Triage, review, merge and release. Cannot grant roles. |
| **Contributor** | Anyone | Open issues and pull requests. |

- The Founder is a Maintainer's only source of access, and may revoke it at any time.
- Decisions are made in public, in issues and PRs. The Founder has the final say.
- If the Founder grants Owner to anyone, GitHub lets that person remove the Founder. So Owner stays with the Founder, and this file does not offer it to others.
- Succession is the Founder's to name here.

This file and the organization's settings govern the project. They do not limit the license: anyone may fork under the AGPL.

Agents work under a person's access. They hold no role.
